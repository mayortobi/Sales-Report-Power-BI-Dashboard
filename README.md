# Sales Report Dashboard w/ Power  BI

![Dashboard Page 1](/images/Sales%20Dashboard%20Page.png)

## Introduction

This Power BI Sales Dashboard was developed to showcase how data can be transformed into meaningful business insights across different sectors of a sales organization. The dashboard provides visibility into overall sales performance, product categories, regional sales, sales representatives, daily and monthly trends, and profitability, enabling users to monitor performance and identify opportunities for growth.

## Skills Showcased

-  **Data Transformation (ETL) with Power Query:** Cleaned, shaped and prepared the raw data for analysis by handling blanks, changing data types and creating new columns.
- **Implict Measured:** Formulated measured to derive key insight and KPIs like `Total Sales` , `Month on Month Growth & diff sales` , `Total Orders` , `Month on Month Growth & diff orders` , `Total quantity sold`  and `Month on Month Growth & diff quantity sold`.
-  **Core Chart:** Utilized **Column, Bar, Line** and **Donut Charts** to compare job counts and track trends over time.
- **KPI Indicator & Tables:** Used **Cards** to display key metrics and **Matrix** to provide granular, sortable data.
- **Dashboard Design:** Designed an intuitive and visually appealling layout, exploring both common and uncommon chart types to best tell the data story.
- **Interactive Reporting:**
  - **Slicers:** To dynamically filter the report by Sales Month.
